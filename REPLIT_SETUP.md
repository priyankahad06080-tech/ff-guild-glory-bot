# Deploy on Replit (Mobile & Desktop)\n\n## Quick Setup (3 steps)\n\n### Step 1: Import to Replit\nGo to: https://replit.com/new?repo=https://github.com/priyankahad06080-tech/ff-guild-glory-bot\n\n### Step 2: Add Discord Token\n1. Click **Secrets** (lock icon) on the left\n2. Add new secret:\n   - Key: `DISCORD_TOKEN`\n   - Value: *your Discord bot token*\n3. Click **Add Secret**\n\n### Step 3: Run\nClick the **Run** button at the top\n\n✅ **Done!** Your bot is now running 24/7\n\n---\n\n## Get Your Discord Token\n\n1. Go to [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications)\n2. Click **New Application**\n3. Go to **Bot** → Click **Add Bot**\n4. Copy the token under **TOKEN**\n\n---\n\n## Invite Bot to Server\n\n1. In Developer Portal → **OAuth2** → **URL Generator**\n2. Select:\n   - Scopes: `bot`\n   - Permissions: `applications.commands`, `send_messages`\n3. Copy & open the generated URL\n4. Select server and authorize\n\n---\n\n## Test Commands\n\nIn your Discord server:\n```\n/glory add @user 100\n/glory view @user\n```\n\n✅ Bot should respond!\n\n---\n\n## Troubleshooting\n\n**Bot not responding?**\n- Check if bot is online in the Replit console\n- Make sure you've invited the bot to your server\n- Verify token is correct\n\n**Need help?** Check the Replit console for errors\n"
